The challenge

Make progress visible

Traditional study practices can make it difficult to get immediate feedback. Work immersion monitoring often adds another layer of paper-based tasks for students and schools.

LearnIQ Track brings these moments together in one friendly, focused system.

The approach

Designed to move learning forward

  • Create AI-generated learning materials with teacher oversight.
  • Give students practice, feedback, and a reason to return.
  • Digitize time-in/out records and daily journal submissions.
  • Give each role the dashboard it needs to take action.
